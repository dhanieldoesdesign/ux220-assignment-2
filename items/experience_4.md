# Unaccompanied teenagers in Singapore

I cannot express how grateful I am to be in a position where it is possible for me to visit a whole new country by myself, or rather, with a friend!

To give some context, my friend was heading to Singapore for a Concert, and asked me if I wanted to tag along. I've always wanted to see this artist as well, and we thought. Hey, we're never going to have the opportunity to do anything like this ever again. Two teenagers, with no adult, in a foreign nation? If that's not an exceptionally rare experience I don't know what is.

We spent 3 days in Singapore. We took the metro to see the typical "Singaporean Sightseeing Destinations". But a highlight of the trip is Sentosa Island's _Luge_ attraction. It's a little pricey, and I'll admit quite touristy. But it is really fun. You get to ride down a track in these wheeled bobsled-like contraptions at honestly probably too fast of speeds. Then, you take a ski lift up! (A ski lift, in Singapore.. a tropical country.)

Another highlight, and I believe the biggest highlight of the trip for me, was Singapore's Metro system. I think this really ousts me as a public transit geek and It's honestly a little embarassing to admit. But I love seeing competent systems working flawlessly. Everything was just so, perfect? You don't appreciate it enough if you have it, but both of us come from a country where smooth, paved roads are pretty much a luxury. Having a competent, fast, and reliable public transit network was just, out of this world. Singapore isn't that large of a nation, and I genuinely believe you can see almost the entire country with just public transit in about a week, and I find that awesome. Not only for tourists, but also for residents alike.

Oh, the concert was awesome too. Lady Gaga was there. B+.
