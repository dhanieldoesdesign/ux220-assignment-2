# River Rafting

I went river rafting in Indonesia in mid 2025! It was fun, you were practically in the middle of the wild and could see near-pristine nature! Outside of the pretty sights, its also just plain fun. Riding rough waves on a small inflatable raft
really does feel like you're completely out of control. It's exhilarating. I remember one time, one of the rafts in our party flipped over after snagging on a rock. Everyone was fine and nobody was in any danger, but it was really funny to watch.

What I will say is that it's definitely not for everyone. It requires you to be at least somewhat physically fit because, believe it or not, staying on a tiny boat thats trying to buck you off the whole time is not easy.

I would rate this experience an A-.
